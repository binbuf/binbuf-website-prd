Dragonfruit Desktop web tour

Dragonfruit provides the system menu glyphs and its three built-in wallpapers.
Default.jpg is the original shipping image. The gallery and settings tiles use
optimized derivatives of Default, Lagoon and Midnight.

The binbuf mark identifies the publisher. The GitHub mark identifies links
to GitHub. Files, Applications, Trash and folder icons are editable WhiteSur
SVG assets as shipped by Dragonfruit. Their corresponding sources, license
and attribution accompany them in ./shell/ and ./native/. The Settings tile
uses the project's bundled Tabler-based artwork. Web, Calendar, Gallery
and Terminal are sample third-party app representations in this browser tour.

Nature photography
./nature/credits.txt lists each chapter photograph, photographer, original
source and license. The images are locally bundled WebP files.

Highland is original artwork generated for the web tour using the
built-in image generation tool. The image brief called for an immersive
wide landscape of overlapping Icelandic volcanic ridges, citron light
grazing velvety green moss, charcoal teal shadows and distant sage mist.
It is a showcase wallpaper for the browser preview.

Generation prompt
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: an original premium operating-system landscape wallpaper, wide 16:9. Create an immersive fine-art landscape photograph of overlapping Icelandic emerald volcanic ridges. Big plunging diagonal velvety moss-covered hills, razor lines of late-afternoon citron sunlight grazing the crest of a large triangular ridge in the right-middle, finely detailed vertical fluting and charcoal teal shadows. Layers of remote dark green ridges turn silvery sage in the atmospheric distance under a thin band of pale mint hazy sky. Dramatic depth and sculptural composition, rich emerald and jade, softly grainy film photography with crisp natural surface detail. Fill the frame with landscape, no trees, no houses, no people, no lettering, graphics, interface or mockup. 2048x1152 landscape.
