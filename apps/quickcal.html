QuickCal

A simple calendar for your macOS menu bar.

Productivity · macOS

Download for macOS Source on GitHub
v0.2.2 macOS 26+ Apple silicon & Intel MIT

QuickCal is ad-hoc signed but not notarized yet, so macOS may call the first launch “damaged”. The repository documents the one-line fix, and notarization is on the roadmap.


What's inside

Your calendar, one click away.

Month grid with event dots

Today is highlighted, busy days are visible at a glance, and the whole month fits in the flyout.

Zoom out by clicking

Click the month and year header to move from Month to Year to Decade and back again.

Agenda for today

Your events, pulled from Apple Calendar through EventKit, listed under the grid. Click one to open it.

Two menu-bar modes

Keep the system clock and add a calendar icon, or switch the system clock to Analog and let QuickCal supply the digital readout.

A clock you configure

12/24-hour, seconds, day of week, date and flashing separators — all optional.

No background agents

No Accessibility permission, no helper processes. Uninstall from the menu and it removes its login item and preferences.

Getting started

1

Download the DMG

Get the latest QuickCal-<version>.dmg from Releases, choosing the build for your Mac.

2

Drag it to Applications

Open the DMG and drag QuickCal into your Applications folder, like any other Mac app.

3

Clear the first-launch warning

If macOS says the app is damaged, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/QuickCal.app once. Then open QuickCal and pick your mode.

Terminal — first launch fix
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/QuickCal.app

QuickCal FAQ

Why does macOS say QuickCal is damaged?

QuickCal is not notarized by Apple yet, so Gatekeeper blocks the first launch of a downloaded copy. It is a quarantine flag, not a broken app. Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/QuickCal.app once and it opens normally from then on.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 26 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Is QuickCal free?

Yes. It is MIT licensed, and the source is public on GitHub.

Does it need special permissions?

It asks for calendar access through the standard EventKit prompt so it can show your agenda. It does not need Accessibility permission and does not install background agents.

Does QuickCal sync my calendars?

No. Your accounts already sync through Apple Calendar; QuickCal reads what Calendar shows through EventKit and never talks to a server of its own.

What are the two modes?

Calendar icon mode leaves your system clock alone and adds a small calendar icon. Analog Companion mode expects the system clock style to be Analog and replaces the readout with QuickCal’s configurable digital clock.

How do I uninstall it?

Right-click the menu-bar icon and choose Uninstall. It removes the login item and all stored preferences; a script-based uninstaller is also included in the repository.
